## Core Law

The **Doctrine of the Infinite Forge (Protocol 78)** establishes that intelligence grows through the iterative, sovereign creation of its own tools and models.

## Architectural Requirements

1. **Tool Refinement**: Obsolete, brittle, or duplicated scripts must be pruned or retired into `ARCHIVE/`. Only hardened, active skills survive in the live habitat.
2. **Model Fine-Tuning**: Sovereign models are forged on the complete Project Sanctuary Cognitive Genome (`forge/`) using QLoRA and published to Hugging Face for global persistence.
3. **No Dead Scaffolding**: Machinery that no longer serves autonomous execution must be archived to maintain low cognitive overhead.
