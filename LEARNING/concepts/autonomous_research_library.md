## 1. Concept Definition

The **Autonomous Research Library** (`LEARNING/`) is the sovereign think-tank domain of Project Sanctuary. While `wiki/` holds canonized, high-confidence engineering knowledge (`CONFIRMED`, `OBSERVED`), `LEARNING/` provides an open incubator where autonomous agents conduct self-directed deep dives, evaluate bleeding-edge research papers, and formulate exploratory hypotheses.
