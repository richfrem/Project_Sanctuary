## Relevance to existing missions

1. **SwarmEvolve (2611.08483)**: none. It concerns LLM-guided evolutionary search, which no current mission covers. MISSION-013 (multi-agent collective intelligence) is only loosely related and does not fit well enough to cite.
2. **Scientist World (2611.08478)**: none. It is a benchmark-style world for AI scientists. It is loosely related to MISSION-010 and MISSION-015, but the abstract offers no testable method for them.
3. **XStack (2611.08472)**: none. It is an ML repo migration benchmark (PyTorch/CUDA vs JAX/TPU). No current mission involves cross-ecosystem migration.
4. **Same Behavior, Different Mechanism (2611.08469)**: MISSION-068 (challenges). It argues that finite experiments cannot certify mechanism-level properties, which bears on whether behavioral checks can verify contract or lifecycle claims. MISSION-006 is a weaker fit.
5. **Scaling activation inversion (2611.08465)**: none. It is an interpretability method for residual-stream directions. No current mission covers interpretability.

## Impact on the project

none. Nothing in the abstracts points to a change in architecture, skills, or practices. The strongest candidate is the caution in paper 4 about verifying mechanism-level claims from finite tests, which the MISSION-068 review should consider. That is a research note, not a change to make now.
