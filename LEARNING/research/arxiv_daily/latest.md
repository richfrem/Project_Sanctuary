### 4. Same Behavior, Different Mechanism: What Finite Experiments Can Certify About Integrated Information

- Link: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08469v1
- Summary: Interpretability, AI auditing and assessments of machine consciousness increasingly rest on mechanism-level quantities: functionals of a system's causal transition law that describe how it computes, not what it outputs.
