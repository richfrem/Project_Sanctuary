## Quick Summary
* **Source:** [arXiv:2610.39302](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39302) (*Proving and Improving the Platonic Representation Hypothesis with JEPAs*, Balestriero & LeCun).
* **Finding:** A 109M-parameter text JEPA trained with "good views" converges to shared multimodal representations 42× faster than autoregressive next-token prediction, outperforming 72B LLMs on vision alignment.
* **Architecture:** Deconstructs the false dichotomy between LLMs and JEPAs. Formulates a "Macro-MoE" architecture where an LLM acts as the orchestrator/communication layer and JEPAs act as domain-native continuous representation engines.
