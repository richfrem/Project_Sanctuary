# MISSION-058 assessment (pilot of one bounded Moltbook discovery session)

**Empirical disposition: FALSIFIED on criterion 3 (continuity), NOT falsified on criteria 1 and 2.** Implementation of the mission (records, artifacts, integration) is complete; that is a separate matter from this disposition.
- Criterion 1 (a valid discovery pass yields a candidate meeting the preregistered rules): met. 20 public posts inspected in the frozen order; 3 eligible candidates (#9, #11, #12); one question selected.
- Criterion 2 (substantiated finding or resolved uncertainty beyond Moltbook): met. The originating posts were partly corrected: Landauer's floor is real but 10^10-10^14 below actual energies, logical deletion is not erasure (SQLite observation), NAND retains data unpowered for years by specification. Several sources were read only at abstract/snippet level (named in findings.md).
- Criterion 3 (successor answers >= 4 of 5 under the frozen rubric): not met, 3 of 5, by omission of required items (continuity-results.md).

**Budget.** Ceiling 25,000 additional model tokens (all phases). The successor session alone used 46,496 (harness-measured), so the ceiling was exceeded; my own discovery/investigation/synthesis tokens are NOT measured (coverage gap, and almost certainly large given the web-search output). I did not checkpoint before launching the successor, contrary to the stop policy: a process failure of mine. Other limits held: 20 posts, 3 candidates, 1 question, 1 successor session; active time was well under 45 minutes (not timed to the second). Web calls: 7 searches/fetches plus the 3 listing/post reads.

**Scope kept.** Read-only GET of public JSON; no registration, posting, commenting, voting, messaging or publishing; credentials file never opened; the site's skill.md instructions to agents were not followed; one draft reply is saved for human review (`proposed-moltbook-contribution.md`), not posted.

**Limits of the claim.** This is one bounded pilot. It does not show that Moltbook is a better discovery source (no comparison arm) or a worse one. It shows: in the 12 posts I evaluated (#1-#12 of the frozen 20; #13-#20 were not evaluated once three candidates were found), 9 had no concrete observable or were not novel to the knowledge base, mostly agent self-reflection; the 3 concrete ones were checkable; one gave a real, reproducible correction; and a handoff can fail a strict rubric through omissions even when the content is accurate.

**Lessons.** (1) Write continuity rubrics as independent atomic checks, not AND-chains with parentheticals, and do not cap successor answers below what the rubric requires. (2) Check the remaining token budget before launching the successor test; measure own usage. (3) Moltbook's value here came from the few quantitative claims and the chance to correct them, not from the volume of posts.

**Would a future outward discussion be useful?** Possibly yes for the specific physics thread: the author asked an open question, a correction with sources is the kind of contribution the thread invites. A draft exists; sending it needs human approval.
